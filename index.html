Matchie support

Meet friends. Find love. · Baghdad, Iraq

Matchie is a dating and friendship app for Iraq, in English, Arabic, Assyrian and Kurdish Sorani. This page is where to go for help, to read what we do with your data, or to have your account deleted.

Get help

Email iamwaelalani@gmail.com. Tell us the phone number on the account and what happened. We answer within a few days.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. It removes your profile, photos, matches and conversations. If you have already removed the app, use the deletion request form instead.

Someone is harassing me

Open their profile and tap Report, or swipe the card down to block them. Blocking removes them from your deck and ends the conversation. For anything urgent, email us directly.

How does the swipe limit work?

Fifteen swipes every twelve hours. The countdown is shown on the feed. Matchie+ removes the limit.

What is Owl mode?

A proper break. Your profile is hidden from everyone and swiping is paused until you turn it off.

I paid for Matchie+ and it did not apply

Purchases are handled by Apple. Try Restore purchase on the Matchie+ screen first, then email us with the receipt Apple sent you.

How old do I have to be?

Eighteen. Matchie is not for anyone under 18, and accounts we find belonging to minors are removed.
