Delete your account

Two ways — the app is faster

If you still have the app

Open Matchie and go to Profile → Settings → Delete account, then confirm. This is immediate. It removes:

  • your profile, photos, bio and interests
  • your matches and every conversation
  • your swipes, blocks, phone number and date of birth
  • the account itself

It cannot be undone, and it does not cancel a Matchie+ subscription — cancel that in your Apple ID settings, or you will keep being charged.

If you have already removed the app

Email us and we will do it for you. You do not need to reinstall anything.

Send an email to [[CONTACT-EMAIL]] with the subject Delete my Matchie account, and include:

  • the phone number the account was created with, in full (+964…)
  • the first name on the profile

We use those two only to find the right account, and we delete them along with it. You will get a confirmation once it is done — within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Email the request
Before submitting to the App Store: replace every [[CONTACT-EMAIL]] on this site with a real, monitored address. Apple rejects a privacy policy that has no working way to reach you. One command from the repo root: grep -rl '\[\[CONTACT-EMAIL\]\]' docs | xargs sed -i '' 's/\[\[CONTACT-EMAIL\]\]/you@yourdomain.com/g'

What we keep, and why

If your account was reported for harassment or fraud, we keep the report and the account identifier for up to 12 months after deletion. That is the only way to stop a banned person from returning the next day. It contains no profile content.

Just want a break instead?

Turn on Owl mode in the app. Your profile is hidden and swiping pauses, and nothing is deleted. Turn it off whenever you want to be seen again.
