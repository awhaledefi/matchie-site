Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

This policy describes what the Matchie iPhone app collects, why, where it is kept, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What we collect

DataWhy
Your phone numberTo secure the account and sign you back in. It is never shown to other people.
First nameShown on your profile.
Date of birthTo confirm you are 18 and to show your age. The date itself is never shown — only the age.
Gender, and who you want to seeTo decide whose profile you are shown. Taken together this can imply sexual orientation, so we treat it as sensitive and never share it.
Photos you addShown on your profile.
Bio, work, city, interests, anthemShown on your profile.
Messages you sendTo deliver them to the person you matched with.
Swipes, matches, blocks and reportsTo run matching and to act on abuse reports.
An anonymous account identifierTo tie the above to your account without using your phone number as the key.

What we do not collect

  • Your precise location. The app does not ask for location access and does not read it.
  • Your contacts. Not requested, not read.
  • Anything about your activity in other apps or on the web. Matchie contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no tracking of any kind. We do not sell or share data with data brokers.
  • Payment details. Purchases go through Apple. We never see your card.

Where it is stored

Your profile, settings and conversations are stored on your iPhone, and on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), which we use as our backend. Photos you add are written to the app's private storage on your device.

Firebase is operated by Google and data may be processed on servers outside Iraq. Google acts as our processor under its own terms.

Who can see what

  • Other users see your name, age, city, distance, photos, bio, work, interests and anthem.
  • Other users never see your phone number, your date of birth, your Instagram or WhatsApp handle, or who you have blocked — unless you send those to someone yourself in a chat.
  • Turning on Owl mode hides your profile from everyone.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Reports of abuse are kept for up to 12 months after the account is gone, so that a banned person cannot simply come back.

Your choices

  • See and correct it. Everything on your profile is editable in the app.
  • Delete it. Profile → Settings → Delete account in the app, or the web form if you no longer have the app installed. Deletion is permanent and takes effect within 30 days.
  • Take it with you. Email us and we will send you a copy of your data.

Age

Matchie is for people 18 and over. We do not knowingly keep data about anyone younger. If you believe a minor is using Matchie, email us and we will remove the account.

Security

Traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit. Access to the backend is restricted to the people who operate the service. No system is perfect; if we ever discover a breach affecting you, we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
